Listening to sonore

Sounds made with sonore, each beside plots of the same audio you hear and the code that made it. Every topic has a page of its own.

Seeing and changing sounds

  • Seeing speech: a short course in time-frequency analysis on one spoken sentence.
  • Cepstral analysis: separating a voice's pitch from its timbre.
  • Modulation spectrogram: how fast and how deeply each band's envelope moves, moment by moment.
  • Analysis and resynthesis: a filterbank that reconstructs exactly, and spectrogram masking.
  • Phase vocoder: how it works, and duration, pitch and partials changed independently.

Stimuli

  • Spectrotemporal ripples: sounds defined by a moving pattern of modulation.
  • Iterated rippled noise: a pitch made from noise and a delay.
  • Binaural cues: differences between the ears: timing, and correlation that changes.
  • Classic stimuli: speech-shaped noise, beats and roughness, binaural beats, tone sequences.
  • Sound textures: recordings and their syntheses from statistics.

Listeners in the world

  • Moving talkers: three talkers rendered through measured HRIRs, one of them moving.
  • Synthetic reverberation: rooms built from the statistics of real ones, and rooms that break them.
  • Hearing through a vocoder: a simulation of cochlear-implant hearing.
Each page is a script in docs/gallery of the sonore repository, run by docs/gallery/build.py.
